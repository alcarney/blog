   I've never felt that I was particualy good at taking notes and have always struggled to really know what to write.
   I still might not really know what to right, but after reading this I think I at least have a better idea of what *not* to write.

   At various points while reading this I found myself nodding along with what was being said as I realised that I did pretty much everything that the book said *not* to do while I was at uni!

   - I would copy down notes from the board verbaitim, which as the book says, can be done with almost no thinking.
   - I would mistake being able to follow the lecturer's explanation for understanding.
